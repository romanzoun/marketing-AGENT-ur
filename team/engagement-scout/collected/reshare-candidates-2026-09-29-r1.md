What happens when the people who understand an old system better than anyone else decide to build the new one?

I’ve been thinking about this a lot lately, especially as tokenization starts moving into more industries.

We talk a lot about middlemen being disrupted, but I think we sometimes miss something important:

The people closest to the problem often have the best understanding of what needs to replace it.

They know the inefficiencies, where trust breaks down, what customers actually struggle with, & the relationships, processes and context that a new technology cannot simply recreate overnight.

That knowledge doesn't become useless because the technology changes.

In many cases, it becomes the foundation for building what comes next.

I wrote about this through the stories of PawaPay, Gojek, Nubank, and PayPal, and what their journeys can teach us about the opportunity sitting in front of people who already understand their industries.

Because the question isn't really: “Will tokenization replace my role?”

A more interesting question is: “What could I build with everything I already know about this market now that this technology exists?”

That's what this piece explores.
