For me, the uncomfortable bit is not that the next privileged user may be software; it is that nobody may feel personally responsible for its keys. An AI agent with standing access is like a keyring that never sleeps—give it an owner, the fewest keys possible, short expiry dates, and a trail humans can review. I would add a qualified signature, so liability is clear, even if someone gives acces to his agent. #Compliance #eIDAS #RecordsManagement
